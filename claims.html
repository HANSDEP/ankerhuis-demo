We'll take it from here.

In danger? Call 112 first. For help on the road or abroad, call the emergency assistance number on your insurance card.

Tell us what happened, or ask Buzz in the chat to take in your claim. Our claims team reviews it and you receive a claim number by email.

  1. Limit further damage
    Where it's safe: turn off the water main, cover a broken window, move the car off the road.
  2. Gather what we need
    What happened, when and where, and photos of the damage.
  3. Add what fits your claim
    Car: the other party's licence plate and a European accident report. Theft or burglary: a police report. Travel: receipts or medical invoices.
  4. Report it
    Through the chat, any time. We confirm we received it and tell you the next step.
